[size=5][b][color=#fff2cc]Description[/color][/b][/size]

[u]Massgate[/u] adds [color=#fff2cc]exotic-matter gates[/color] to Icarus: mid-to-late-game fast travel that stays a choice, not a free ride.

A gate is a pair. The [b][color=#9fd3ff]Anchor Lattice[/color][/b] is the heavy, power-hungry base end; the [b][color=#ffd39f]Resonator[/color][/b] is the light field end. Tune both to the same colour charge ([color=#ff6b6b]Red[/color], [color=#7ee787]Green[/color] or [color=#79b8ff]Blue[/color]), engage, wait three seconds, and step out at the other end. Going [b]out[/b] burns Exotics loaded into the Anchor; coming [b]home[/b] is free. Tames set to Follow travel with you.

[i]There are exactly three colour charges and no fourth. Anchors of all three colours can stand together at base; a Resonator refuses to tune with any other gate within 500 m, so outposts stay far apart.[/i]

[line]

[size=5][b][color=#fff2cc]Installation[/color][/b][/size]

Two parts, both required. Needs [b]UE4SS 3.0.1[/b] installed first.

[list=1]
[*]Copy [code]Massgate_v<version>_P.pak[/code] into [code]Icarus\Content\Paks\mods\[/code] (lowercase [code]mods[/code], create it if missing; remove any older Massgate pak).
[*]Copy the [code]Massgate[/code] folder into [code]Icarus\Binaries\Win64\ue4ss\Mods\[/code].
[*]Start the game. The "Mods Detected" dialog lists the pak; [code]ue4ss\UE4SS.log[/code] shows [code]Massgate v... loaded[/code].
[/list]

[b]In game:[/b] unlock [b]Massgate[/b] in the Tier 4 Fabricator tree (next to the Exotic Processor), craft an Anchor and a Resonator, place them 500 m+ apart with power. Alternate-interact opens the [b]Lattice controls[/b] wheel to pick a colour. Hold interact on the Anchor to load Exotics. Press interact to engage.

[b]Multiplayer:[/b] everyone needs the pak and the Lua; dedicated servers must run UE4SS (Windows only).

[line]

[size=5][b][color=#fff2cc]Main features[/color][/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]Paired gates[/b], one pair per colour, three colours per prospect.
[*][b]Asymmetric cost:[/b] outbound costs Exotics, inbound is free.
[*][b]Real constraints:[/b] power on both ends, 500 m interference, 3 s charge-up, cooldown.
[*][b]Tames on Follow[/b] come along; your farm stays put.
[*][b]Lattice panel:[/b] four Exotics slots plus one module slot.
[*][b]Phase Coupler module:[/b] the Anchor powers its Resonator over the colour channel, at a higher trip cost.
[*][b]Control wheel[/b] for colour, power and pick-up; coloured [b]map and compass icons[/b].
[/list]
